Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Love Quackers is a word game designed to be played without creating an account or providing personal information. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the Love Quackers mobile app.

Information you provide

The game does not include account registration, advertising, purchases, contact forms, or online social features. Your letter guesses and current puzzle progress are handled on your device and are not intentionally collected or stored by Love Quackers. Secret answers and hints entered in Pass & Play mode remain on your device and may be temporarily saved to recover an interrupted game. They are replaced when another puzzle begins.

Technical information

Love Quackers is designed to operate locally on your device without requiring a continuous internet connection. The game does not intentionally collect or transmit IP addresses, device identifiers, or usage analytics to its developer. Third-party services used to distribute the app or host this privacy policy may independently process limited technical information, such as IP addresses, device types, request times, and security logs, according to their own privacy practices.

Cookies, analytics, and advertising

Love Quackers does not intentionally use advertising trackers, third-party analytics, or cookies for behavioral tracking. If these practices change, this policy and the Google Play Data safety information will be updated before the change is released.

Children's privacy

Love Quackers does not knowingly request or collect personal information from children. Because the game has no accounts, public posting, or in-game messaging, players should not submit personal information through the game.

Data sharing

Love Quackers does not sell or intentionally share personal information collected through gameplay. Third-party services responsible for distributing the app or hosting this privacy policy may process technical information independently under their own privacy policies.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the game or its data practices change. The latest version will remain available on this page with its updated date.

Contact

Questions about Love Quackers or this privacy policy can be sent to contact.lovequackers@gmail.com.
