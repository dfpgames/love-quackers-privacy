Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

Love Quackers is a word game designed to be played without creating an account or providing personal information. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the website or installed app.

Information you provide

The game does not include account registration, advertising, purchases, contact forms, or online social features. Your letter guesses and current puzzle progress are handled on your device and are not intentionally collected or stored by Love Quackers. Secret answers and hints entered in Pass & Play mode stay in this browser tab to resume after an unexpected reload, and are replaced when another puzzle begins.

Technical information

The hosting services used to deliver and protect the game may automatically process limited technical information, such as an IP address, browser or device type, request time, and security or error logs. This information is used to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service.

Cookies, analytics, and advertising

Love Quackers does not intentionally use advertising trackers, third-party analytics, or cookies for behavioral tracking. If these practices change, this policy and the Google Play Data safety information will be updated before the change is released.

Children's privacy

Love Quackers does not knowingly request or collect personal information from children. Because the game has no accounts, public posting, or in-game messaging, players should not submit personal information through the game.

Data sharing

Love Quackers does not sell personal information. Limited technical information may be processed by the infrastructure provider as necessary to host, secure, and deliver the game.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the game or its data practices change. The latest version will remain available on this page with its updated date.

Contact

Questions about Love Quackers or this privacy policy can be sent to contact.lovequackers@gmail.com.
